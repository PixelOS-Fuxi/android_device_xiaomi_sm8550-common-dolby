## All proprietary files listed here are from Xiaomi 13 (fuxi)
## OS3.0.308.0.WMCCNXM, matching the rest of the sm8550-common vendor tree.
##
## This tree owns the Dolby *audio* stack only. The Dolby Vision video components
## (bin/hw/dolbycodec2, lib64/c2.dolby.hevc.*, lib64/c2.dolby.store.so,
## lib64/c2.dolby.client.so, lib64/libdolbyottcameracontrol.so) live in
## device/xiaomi/fuxi-miuicamera, because they are only reachable through
## MIUI Camera and their Codec2 xml declarations sit next to the camera blobs.
##
## libcodec2_store_dolby.so is deliberately owned by *this* tree: the Dolby
## audio decoders (libcodec2_soft_ac4dec.so, libcodec2_soft_ddpdec.so) and the
## media.c2 service all resolve android::GetCodec2DolbyComponentStore() from it
## to expose the IComponentStore/default1 instance.
